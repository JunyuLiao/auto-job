# Recommended company set

The company list in `config/portals.yml` is deliberately broader than a
single “dream company” list. It mixes brand value, technical match, and the
chance of owning meaningful systems work.

| Group | Companies | Why they are relevant |
|---|---|---|
| Big tech / platforms | Google, Microsoft, Meta, Apple, NVIDIA, Amazon/AWS, AMD, Intel, Qualcomm, Cisco, IBM, Oracle, Salesforce | Large-scale distributed systems, GPU/accelerator software, cloud infrastructure, and strong internship programs |
| AI labs / model infrastructure | OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, Together AI, Fireworks AI, Modal, Anyscale, Baseten, Hugging Face, Weights & Biases | Inference, model serving, ML platforms, Ray, evaluation, and developer infrastructure |
| AI hardware / compute | Cerebras, Groq, d-Matrix, Tenstorrent, SambaNova, CoreWeave, Crusoe, Lambda | Compilers, runtimes, kernels, GPU/accelerator fleets, and high-performance inference |
| Data / systems infrastructure | Snowflake, Datadog, Cloudflare, Confluent, MongoDB, ClickHouse, MotherDuck, Cockroach Labs, Pinecone, Weaviate, Redis, Elastic, Temporal, Grafana Labs, Stripe | Databases, real-time analytics, observability, distributed data planes, vector search, and reliable production systems |

This is a watchlist, not a claim that every company has an open Summer 2027
internship today. Career pages and ATS boards are checked at scan time, and
the posting itself remains the source of truth for dates, eligibility, and
intern status. Anthropic is included as a watch target because its current
careers FAQ says it does not currently offer internships; that can change and
the configured web-search handoff makes the status explicit rather than
silently treating the company as an ATS feed.
